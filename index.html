Support & FAQ

1. Does CatLock require complicated system permissions?

No! CatLock uses a 100% App Store Sandbox-compliant overlay window to safely block accidental input without system-wide hooks.

2. How do I unlock my keyboard?

Press Command + Option + U (⌘ + ⌥ + U), or tap Space 5 times rapidly within 2 seconds (holding Spacebar will never unlock).

3. How do I restore purchased Skins or Bundle Upgrades?

Open CatLock from the menu bar and click "Restore Purchases" above the skin selector.

Privacy Policy (CatLock & Clean)

KitSpark Studio respects your privacy. CatLock & Clean runs 100% offline on your Mac with zero servers, zero analytics, and zero third-party tracking SDKs.

  • Sandbox-Compliant Input Lock: Key and click interception occurs strictly inside the active lock overlay window to prevent accidental presses.
  • No Keylogging or Data Collection: Keystrokes are never saved to disk or sent over any network. Stomp counts live purely in temporary memory and clear when closed.
  • In-App Purchases: Processed securely by Apple StoreKit. We never access or store your payment information.
